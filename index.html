A Scam Can Look Like a Normal Message

Scammers target first-time and elderly investors using guaranteed-return claims, fake regulator logos, and intense urgency.

FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION CONTENT
"Congratulations! You have been selected for a special investment opportunity.

Get Guaranteed 40% profit in 7 days!
Send ₹20,000 within 30 minutes to lock your seat."

What ScamShield Notices:

  1. Guaranteed return claim: Market investments cannot legally promise fixed returns.
  2. Urgency: Short deadlines panic users into acting before verifying.
  3. Payment request: Asks for money transfers to unknown personal accounts.

Learn to Spot Warning Signs

💰 Guaranteed Returns

Nobody can honestly guarantee that an investment will make a fixed profit in stock markets.

⏳ Urgency & Pressure

"Being told to act in 30 minutes can stop you from checking official facts or asking family."

🏛️ Authority Claims

A message saying 'SEBI approved' does not prove that the sender or organization is genuine.

💳 Payment Requests

Be careful when someone asks you to transfer money to personal UPI IDs before verification.

🔒 Credential Theft

Never share OTPs, PINs, bank passwords, or install screen-sharing apps like AnyDesk.

📱 Fake Trading Apps & Gurus

Social media stock tip groups and unverified APK links often impersonate trusted platforms.
